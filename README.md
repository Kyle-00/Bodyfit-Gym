# BodyFit Gym Website

## Project Brief

A static website for **BodyFit Gym**, a modern fitness and wellness center based in Nairobi, Kenya. The site combines professional fitness training with modern wellness culture, offering visitors insight into the gym’s programs, trainers, facilities, and membership registration system.

# Overview

BodyFit Gym is a Nairobi-based fitness center focused on helping individuals achieve healthier lifestyles through strength training, cardio programs, nutrition coaching, and wellness support. This website acts as the gym’s online presence, showcasing its services, trainers, facilities, and membership enquiry form.

---

# Features

- **Hero Section** — A motivational value proposition introducing the gym’s mission and fitness philosophy.
- **Services Section** — Highlights core services including Personal Training, Group Fitness Classes, and Nutrition Coaching.
- **Team Section** — Profiles of certified fitness trainers and wellness coaches with an embedded gym workout video.
- **Membership Form** — A structured contact and membership enquiry form with client-side validation including:
  - Required field enforcement
  - Email format validation
  - Kenyan phone number validation (`0712345678`)
  - Fitness goals
  - Preferred training program selection
- **Footer** — Social media links (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter) and copyright information.

## Accessibility

This project was built with accessibility best practices:

-Semantic HTML elements (<header>, <main>, <section>, <article>, <footer>)
-ARIA labels on navigation and social media links
-`alt` text for all images
-Keyboard-accessible navigation
-Proper form labels connected with for and id
-Logical heading hierarchy (h1 → h2 → h3)

## Business Rationale

The BodyFit Gym website was designed to support business growth and customer engagement by:

1. Building Brand Identity

The modern design and strong visual presentation establish BodyFit Gym as a professional and motivating fitness brand.

2. Increasing Membership Enquiries

The membership contact form allows potential clients to easily reach out and inquire about training programs and memberships.

3. Improving User Experience

Responsive design ensures visitors can access the website seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

4. Showcasing Services

Dedicated service sections help communicate the gym’s offerings including:

-Strength training
-Group fitness
-Cardio programs
-Nutrition coaching
5. Enhancing Accessibility & SEO

Accessibility features and SEO-friendly structure improve usability and online visibility.
